FAQ

Who can participate?

Anyone can apply! We accept applications in August. We're looking for builders, founders, creators, engineers, and community builders.

Does it cost anything to attend?

No, attendance is free thanks to our amazing sponsors.

What can I win?

The grand prize is a shower, yes, you heard that right. Imagine having that on your LinkedIn.

There will also be sponsor-specific prizes. If you used a sponsor's technology, you qualify for their track. More info on this coming soon.

Are there travel reimbursements?

No.

Is there a Showerhacks group chat?

Yes — join our Discord: discord.gg/4xEtJc9xqZ
